ABOUT

What is MCPE GALAXY?

A PvP testing hub built for one reason — to find out who's actually good, and give them the rank to prove it.

The point of the server

No fake elo, no boosted ranks. Every tier on MCPE GALAXY is earned through 1v1 tests against players of your own level. Lose, learn, retest. Simple.

Java + Bedrock, same rules

Both platforms get their own ladder and their own tier bracket. Cross-platform players can be ranked on both — completely separate, completely fair.

Built for testers

Ranked arenas, recorded fights, match tracking and clean fair matchmaking. Your skill does the talking — the system just keeps score.
